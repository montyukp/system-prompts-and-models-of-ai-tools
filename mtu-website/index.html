What I build and look after

Two things, not six.

I don't offer a long menu of services. I do two things well, and I look after them for you on an ongoing basis so they don't go stale.

Website Build

A clean, simple website — a homepage, a page about what you do, and a way for people to contact you. No bloat, no pages that don't earn their place. Built to load fast and make sense on a phone, since that's how most people will find it.

Simple Automations

Small pieces of setup that save you time — things like auto-replying to enquiries, sending booking reminders, connecting a contact form straight to email or a spreadsheet, or sorting incoming leads. Explained plainly, set up once, left running quietly in the background.

The Ongoing Bit

Both come with a monthly management fee. That covers hosting, keeping things working, small updates, and adjusting the automations as the business changes — instead of a one-off job that goes stale in six months.
