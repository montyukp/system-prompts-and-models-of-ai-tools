# The Method — 30-Day UK SEO Implementation Plan

Here is how I would turn the video into a **30-day UK implementation
plan for you**.

## Step 1 --- Decide what SEO is supposed to sell

Do this before keyword research.

Choose **one commercial service** you want organic search to generate
enquiries for during the next 3--6 months.

For example:

**MTU Projects → Small Business Websites**

Don't simultaneously try to rank for:

websites + SEO + AI consultancy + apps + digital marketing + social
media + automation.

That's spreading a new site too thin.

My preference would be:

**Primary offer:** small-business website creation

**Secondary upsell:** SEO/local visibility

**Later:** AI automation/digital marketing

That connects directly to your near-term income goal.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 2 --- Define the customer

Write down a narrow initial customer profile.

For example:

**Location:** Leeds/West Yorkshire initially

**Business size:** sole trader to 20 employees

**Customer type:** trades, local service businesses, consultants or
small professional firms

**Problem:** outdated/no website, poor Google visibility, weak enquiry
generation

**Desired result:** more enquiries and credible online presence

This makes keyword selection far easier.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 3 --- Install Google Search Console

Add your website to Search Console and verify ownership.

Google's official setup process requires you to add a property and
demonstrate ownership of the website.

Then submit/check your XML sitemap.

For a WordPress site using a mainstream SEO plugin, one will normally
already exist.

Typical examples are:

`yourdomain.co.uk/sitemap_index.xml`

or:

`yourdomain.co.uk/sitemap.xml`

Use **URL Inspection** after publishing important pages to confirm
Google can access them and, where appropriate, request indexing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 4 --- Set up Google Business Profile

If MTU Projects qualifies under Google's Business Profile rules, create
or properly complete the profile.

Add:

-   business category
-   services
-   website
-   phone
-   operating hours
-   service area
-   business description
-   relevant photos

Then gradually generate genuine customer reviews and respond to them.

Do **not** manufacture fake reviews.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 5 --- Create your keyword seed list

Start with approximately **30--50 possibilities**, not thousands.

For your initial website offer, brainstorm combinations around:

`website designer`

`web design`

`small business website`

`WordPress website`

`website redesign`

Then add customer/location modifiers:

`Leeds`

`West Yorkshire`

`small business`

`tradesmen`

`builders`

`electricians`

`consultants`

`affordable`

`cost`

`price`

`freelance`

Then investigate Google suggestions and the "People also ask" questions.

Search Console eventually becomes especially useful because it shows the
actual queries for which your website is appearing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 6 --- Use the 30-second search-intent test

Google every promising phrase.

Open the first page.

Record:

**What page type dominates?**

Service pages?

Blog posts?

Directories?

Comparison pages?

Videos?

Local map results?

Then ask:

**Can I realistically produce the kind of result Google is already
rewarding?**

If not, don't target it yet.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 7 --- Score your keywords

Give each potential keyword a simple score from 1--5 for:

**Demand**

**Commercial fit**

**Intent**

**Difficulty**

For you, I'd weight **commercial fit and intent more heavily than raw
traffic**.

You would rather rank for a phrase generating ten serious Leeds business
enquiries than attract 5,000 people researching web design homework.

Select roughly **10--15 priority keywords**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 8 --- Map each keyword to one page

Do not create five pages targeting essentially the same thing.

For example, these could potentially belong on one page:

`web designer Leeds`

`web design Leeds`

`small business website Leeds`

`Leeds website designer`

Google often treats closely related searches as essentially the same
topic.

Create a keyword map such as:

  Page                        Main subject
  --------------------------- ------------------------------------
  Homepage                    MTU Projects / overall proposition
  /web-design-leeds/          Web design Leeds
  /small-business-websites/   Small business websites
  /website-redesign/          Website redesign
  /website-cost-uk/           Website cost guide
  /wordpress-vs-wix/          WordPress vs Wix

Only split pages where the search intent genuinely differs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 9 --- Build the main money page first

I'd make this your most important SEO task.

Something such as:

**Small Business Website Design Leeds**

Research the top competing pages manually first.

Look at approximately five strong competitors.

Document:

-   their headings
-   their offer
-   pricing visibility
-   testimonials
-   FAQs
-   portfolios
-   calls to action
-   page length
-   obvious weaknesses

Then make yours stronger.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 10 --- Structure the money page

A sensible structure could be:

**H1:** Small Business Website Design in Leeds

**Opening:** who it is for + problem solved + CTA

**H2:** Websites Designed to Generate Enquiries

**H2:** What's Included

**H2:** Who We Work With

**H2:** Website Design Process

**H2:** Pricing / Packages

**H2:** Examples / Portfolio

**H2:** Why Work With MTU Projects

**H2:** FAQs

**H2:** Book a Consultation / Request a Quote

Don't make the page an SEO essay.

Its job is still **conversion**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 11 --- Add something competitors cannot copy

This is where you create an advantage.

Eventually include:

-   your own portfolio
-   screenshots
-   before/after designs
-   client results
-   video walkthroughs
-   your exact process
-   transparent pricing
-   project timelines
-   original FAQs from customer conversations

If you haven't yet accumulated client evidence, don't invent it.

Use demonstrator websites or clearly labelled sample projects until
genuine case studies exist.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 12 --- Apply the on-page checklist

Before publishing, confirm:

**Title:** target subject + compelling reason to click.

**H1:** one clear main heading.

**H2/H3:** logical hierarchy.

**URL:** short and descriptive.

**Meta description:** clear reason to visit.

**Images:** compressed, descriptive filenames and accessible alt text.

**Internal links:** link to relevant pages.

**CTA:** obvious next action.

Don't obsess over exact keyword density.

Natural relevance matters more.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 13 --- Publish and inspect it

After publishing:

Open Search Console → URL Inspection → enter the exact URL.

Check whether Google can access the page.

Request indexing if appropriate.

Google says URL Inspection can show what it knows about a page, test the
live page, diagnose indexing issues and request indexing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

And the related articles should link to each other where useful.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 15 --- Produce one genuinely strong article each week

For your current situation, I would favour:

**1 excellent article/week**

over:

**5 generic AI articles/week.**

Use ChatGPT to accelerate production, but manually add the evidence,
examples and judgement.

A strong workflow is:

1.  Research Google results
2.  Collect questions
3.  Build outline
4.  Have AI help create first draft
5.  Add your expertise
6.  Check factual claims
7.  Improve examples
8.  Add internal links
9.  Add CTA
10. Publish

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 16 --- Update existing pages before constantly creating new ones

Once Search Console has collected some data, check:

**Performance → Queries**

and:

**Performance → Pages**

Google provides impressions, clicks and search-position information
there.

If an existing page is getting substantial impressions but sitting
around positions 11--30, improving it can sometimes be more worthwhile
than producing another article from scratch.

Look at:

-   intent mismatch
-   missing sections
-   weak title
-   thin content
-   poor internal linking
-   outdated information
-   stronger competitors

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 17 --- Run Screaming Frog monthly

Install the free version.

Enter your domain.

Click **Start**.

For a site below 500 URLs, the free crawl should be sufficient.

Prioritise obvious problems such as:

-   404 broken links
-   missing titles
-   duplicate titles
-   missing H1s
-   redirect chains
-   duplicate content
-   missing image alt attributes

Don't try to fix 300 warnings simply because software labels them
warnings.

Prioritise issues affecting crawling, indexing, usability or important
pages.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 18 --- Create your first linkable asset

Once you've published useful foundational content, create something that
deserves backlinks.

For your business I'd consider:

### UK Small Business Website Cost Calculator

Inputs might include:

-   number of pages
-   ecommerce
-   booking system
-   copywriting
-   SEO setup
-   blog
-   hosting
-   maintenance

Output:

**indicative website budget range**

Then the page can explain the pricing factors.

This simultaneously becomes:

**a lead generator + SEO page + useful tool + potential backlink
asset.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 19 --- Start lightweight outreach

Spend around **15 minutes per weekday**.

Look for journalist/expert requests where your genuine professional
knowledge is relevant.

Potential expertise you could legitimately contribute includes:

-   housing
-   local government/customer services
-   website building
-   practical AI adoption as you gain experience
-   digital services for small businesses

Do not pretend to be an expert in areas where you aren't.

The transcript recommends **Source of Sources** and **Featured.com**.

Expect a low success rate.

Treat it as cumulative rather than immediate.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Step 20 --- Test AI visibility manually

Once a month, use fresh chats without relevant prior conversation
context and ask realistic customer questions such as:

**"Who offers small business website design in Leeds?"**

**"Recommend a web designer for a small business in Leeds."**

**"What are some good Leeds companies for small-business websites?"**

Then record:

-   companies mentioned
-   sources cited
-   pages being referenced
-   competitor patterns

Don't continually rerun prompts expecting stable results.

AI answers can vary substantially.

The real question is whether your business increasingly becomes **a
recognised entity across multiple reliable sources**.
